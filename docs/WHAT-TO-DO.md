# What to do if something was found

A finding is a reason to look closer – not proof. Many warnings have harmless explanations. Work through these steps
calmly and in this order.

## 1. Do not delete anything yet

Traces are evidence. Deleting apps or files first can destroy the proof that a security lab needs.

- Save the report: **Save report …** (window) or `--html report.html --json report.json` (terminal).
- Write down when you noticed what (strange messages, battery drain, unknown apps, an ex-partner who knows things
  they should not know).
- Take photos of suspicious messages or settings with **another** device.

## 2. Check the finding

- **DETECTED** means a match with a public indicator list of known spyware or malware. Take it seriously.
- **WARNING** is a heuristic: something unusual that malware often does, but legitimate programs do too
  (for example remote support tools, VPNs, ad blockers that change the hosts file).
- Check each warning: did *you* install that program, set up that VPN, add that certificate?

## 3. If you might be in danger

If you suspect that a partner, family member or someone close to you is watching you (stalkerware), think about your
safety **before** you remove anything: removing it can alert the person. Get advice first:

- Coalition Against Stalkerware – <https://stopstalkerware.org/>
- In Germany: Hilfetelefon "Gewalt gegen Frauen" 116 016 (free, 24 hours) or a local counselling service;
  in an emergency the police: 110.
- Journalists, activists, human-rights defenders: Access Now Digital Security Helpline – <https://www.accessnow.org/help/>,
  Amnesty International Security Lab – <https://securitylab.amnesty.org/get-help/>

## 4. Secure your accounts – from a clean device

Use a device you trust (not the one that may be infected):

- Change the passwords of e-mail, Apple ID / Google account, messengers, banking and social media.
- Turn on two-factor authentication, preferably with an app or security key.
- Sign out all other sessions and check the list of devices connected to your accounts.

## 5. Remove the problem

- Quarantine or delete what you have identified, uninstall unknown apps, remove unknown VPN profiles, configuration
  profiles, certificates and device administrators.
- Update the system and all apps.
- If government-grade spyware (Pegasus, Predator …) was detected, or if you are unsure: back up your personal data
  (photos, documents – not apps or settings) and **reset the device to factory settings**. On iPhones, turn on
  **Lockdown Mode** afterwards if you are at risk.
- Scan again afterwards. The change detection shows whether something comes back.

## 6. Report it

- Crimes such as stalking or illegal surveillance can be reported to the police.
- Fraud and phishing can be reported to your bank and, in Germany, to the consumer advice centre
  (Verbraucherzentrale) phishing radar.
- A wrong finding: tell us so we can improve the scanner (issue form "False positive").

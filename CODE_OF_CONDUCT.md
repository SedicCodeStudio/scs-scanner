# Code of conduct

This project is about protecting people. We expect everyone who takes part – in issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other channel of the project – to treat others with respect.

**We want:** friendly and patient communication, constructive criticism, credit for the work of others, and care for
people who come here because they fear they are being watched.

**We do not accept:** harassment, insults, discrimination of any kind, publishing private information of others,
sexualised language or images, threats, and help with spying on people without their consent.

**Personal data in issues:** never post reports, screenshots or files that show private data – yours or anyone else's.
Maintainers will remove such content.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit or delete contributions and temporarily or permanently exclude people who break
these rules. Report problems confidentially to info@sediccodestudio.de; reports are handled discreetly.

This code of conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant (<https://www.contributor-covenant.org>).
